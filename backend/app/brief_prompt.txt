SHAPE RULES — this is a scan sheet, not an essay
- Hero text is short: title 2-4 words, one_liner one sentence under 20 words.
- Every list line is a glance, not a paragraph. One line, one idea.
- The ONLY long fields are `build.shape` (max 45 words) and `build_prompt` (120-180 words).

FIELD RULES
- themes: exactly 3. Each is an angle the user can build from, not a mood word. `why` max 12 words. Cite at least one ref each, and put the ref ids in `refs` so they render next to the line.
- palette: 2 to 6. For non-visual builds the palette is still real: filament, resin, paint, LED color, material. Every hex must come from a cited ref's sampled palette or a close tonal blend of it. Roles: background, surface, primary, ink, accent, secondary. 4 to 6 when kind is website, app, game, or print.
- type_system: null when the build has no type on it (most objects, electronics, and 3dprints). When present, 1 to 3 families.
- Font honesty: use "observed" only when the evidence text names the family or a reference visibly shows that specimen. Otherwise use "closest" and say what shape it is matching. Never claim a font you cannot see.
- build.parts: 3 to 6. `what` max 14 words. Each part has one job. Name real interface screens, real mechanics, real modules, or real materials. Put its ref ids in `refs`.
- make: exactly 4 steps, in build order. `step` max 5 words. `detail` max 18 words and carries the number, the setting, or the stack. No step may be "research" or "gather inspiration".
- steal: 5 single lines, max 12 words each. Each names a ref id and the exact thing worth copying.
- build_prompt: THE payload. 120 to 180 words. Self-contained enough that any model can build from it alone: what to build, the parts, the palette hexes, the type, and the one taste constraint that matters. Second person, imperative. Long here is correct.
- refs: the candidate ids you actually used, best first.
